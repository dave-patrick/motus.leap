Configure the Android app and server

Your YouTube library, organised deliberately.

motus.leap combines a YouTube-library service with a native Android companion. It helps you see what is in your collection, find items that need attention and review where videos should go.

  1. Connect your own service

    Configure the motus.leap backend and its YouTube authorisation, then enter the server address in the Android app and sign in with Google. App sign-in and the backend’s access to YouTube are separate setup steps.

  2. Inspect the library

    Browse playlists and channels, inspect mappings and use the dashboard to queue library, duplicate or misplaced-video scans. Queuing a job does not mean that it has completed.

  3. Review a proposed move

    Inspect an AI suggestion and its destination playlist. Confirm the change only when it is right. AI sorting supports your decision; suggestions are not a promise of automatic or error-free organisation.

What runs where?

The Android app is the companion interface. The configured backend holds the library workflow, talks to YouTube and performs queued work. Downloading the APK alone does not create a working server or authorise a YouTube account.

Current availability

Android 0.5.2 is a development build. Configure your own service and provider access using the setup guide. The board above is an interactive explanation and never changes your account.
