Set the destination once.
Let channel rules do the routing.

When you regularly save videos from the same creators, you shouldn’t need to choose the same playlist every time. Channel-to-playlist mappings connect a channel to the destination you want its videos to use.

Manage those mappings in the web interface and use them in your library workflows. Keep familiar channels on a predictable path, then turn to AI for videos that need a different kind of sorting.

The Android companion lets you inspect your mappings; editing them happens on the web.

AI helps you choose.
You approve the move.

Start with a video in a source playlist. The server sends its title, channel, and description to your configured AI provider and returns a suggested playlist.

Review the result, choose a destination, and confirm. If the suggestion is ambiguous or doesn’t resolve to a known playlist, it cannot trigger a move. You can choose another destination or leave the video where it is.

After approval, the operation screen reports progress and success or failure counts. A suggestion, an approved request, and a completed move are separate stages.

Your service.
Your connected library.

The Android app is the companion. Your backend connects the pieces and carries out the work.

Android

Browse, queue scans, and review suggestions from native Android screens. Enter your server’s HTTPS address and use Google sign-in to connect.

Your backend

The self-hosted service stores the YouTube™ credentials, processes background jobs, and connects to your chosen AI provider. It needs to be running and reachable for the app to work.

YouTube™ & AI access

Signing in to the app and authorising the backend to access YouTube™ are separate steps. AI sorting also needs a configured provider; video metadata is sent to that provider for classification.

A little setup.
A more useful library.

Start with the service, then connect your phone.
The setup guide covers the configuration.

  1. Prepare the backend

    Set up the motus.leap server with HTTPS, configure Google OAuth, and connect your YouTube™ account. Add your AI provider if you want sorting suggestions.

  2. Connect the Android app

    Install the APK on Android 8.0 or newer, enter the server address, and continue with Google in your system browser.

  3. Explore before making changes

    Check the dashboard and browse your playlists. Queue a scan, inspect its findings, and confirm only the changes you want.

Before you download

Does the APK include a server?

No. The APK connects to an existing motus.leap backend. You’ll need to configure and run that service separately.

Will AI sorting move videos automatically?

The reviewed Android sorting flow requires your explicit confirmation. Inspect the proposed playlist or select another destination before approving a move.

Can I use it without an AI provider?

AI suggestions require a configured provider. Library browsing and scan workflows use the backend’s YouTube™ connection separately.

What should I expect from this release?

Android 0.5.2 is a development build. YouTube™ permissions, API quota, server availability, and provider configuration affect which operations can run. Use the operation results to verify changes.
